Privacy Policy

Extension: Convert MOV to MP4
Last updated: September 16, 2026

1. Overview

Convert MOV to MP4 is a Chrome browser extension that converts video files between the MOV and MP4 containers directly inside your browser. This privacy policy describes what data we collect, how we use it, and with whom it is shared.

2. Data We Collect

None of your video content ever leaves your device. The extension opens a single tab, and all conversion (remuxing or transcoding) happens locally in your browser using the WebCodecs API and the open-source Mediabunny library. There is no backend server, no upload, and no network request involved in converting a file.

  • Video files: Read locally from disk (via drag-and-drop or the file picker), converted locally, and either downloaded to your Downloads folder or streamed to a location you choose. We never see, receive, or store your files.
  • Star rating: If you rate the extension 4-5 stars, we open the Chrome Web Store review page for this extension in a new tab. Nothing is sent to us. If you rate it 1-3 stars, an optional feedback box lets you write a comment; it is not transmitted anywhere unless a future version of the extension adds a way to send it, which would update this policy first.

3. Permissions

The extension requests no special Chrome permissions. It only opens its own converter page when you click the toolbar icon.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit any data, because we do not collect any.

5. Third Parties

The only outbound navigation the extension ever triggers is opening the Chrome Web Store review page in a new tab if you choose to rate 4-5 stars — governed by Google's privacy policy.

6. Your Rights

Since we collect nothing, there is nothing stored about you to access, export, or delete.
